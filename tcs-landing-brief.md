# Surface brief — TCS landing page

Scope: standalone marketing/landing page (`tcs-landing.html`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: Thai Cardfight!! Vanguard sellers and buyers currently trading in Facebook groups. Job: decide TCS is worth moving to. Proof/content: the escrow money-flow state machine and anti-fraud rules from [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md); no invented testimonials, prices, or user counts. Constraint: no backend exists yet, so any CTA must not promise a working signup.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The escrow flip — trust is proven physically as the card turns face-up, not asserted in a headline or badge row; refuses the trust-badge/testimonial-carousel marketing template.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black ground (`#0A0C10` base, `#12161D` panels), Vanguard blue `#2F8FE8` primary accent, circuit-cyan `#5FD4FF` glow/motion accent, card white `#F4F5F7` headline text, steel `#8C93A3` secondary/borders. Space Grotesk (headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (prices, statuses, timers only). Card motif: rounded-rect card shapes with one glowing icon (burst/star/sparkle) as background decoration; a two-card stacked star mark as the brand icon. Real card-back circuit-glow pattern authored as the hero object.

**STORY:** Visitor understands TCS is a Thai card marketplace where the app itself holds the money until the buyer approves the card, not the seller and not a Facebook comment thread; scrolls from name → the Facebook-group problem → how escrow/unboxing-video works → wants in.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed near-black hero, small stacked-card brand mark top-left, Space Grotesk headline stating the mechanism in plain terms, one real hero card object (card-back proportions, circuit glow) as the signature object, primary action below the fold line.

**FORM:** Shaped directly from the user's own pinned reference images (card-back photo, decoration-card icons, stacked-card mark) and stated content brief (name → what is TCS → how we work, scroll-revealed, card-flip signature animation); concept-seed skipped as a precisely specified, brief-pinned request per new-work.md §3.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
